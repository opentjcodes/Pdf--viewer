<i>MJ PDF</i> is a fast, simply, yet powerful and totally free PDF reader. It pairs a powerful reader with a full library manager, and it respects your privacy.

<b>Library</b>

* Home screen with Recent, Library and Folders tabs.
* Continue reading right where you left off, with a detailed reading history.
* Organize with reading statuses (to-read, reading, completed and more).
* Browse your device like a file manager, including SD cards.
* Search every PDF on your device.

<b>Reader</b>

* Fast and smooth, with dark mode for the app and the PDF itself.
* True full screen with configurable shortcut buttons.
* Highlight text, add notes, and save them into the PDF.
* Draw handwritten signatures and save them into the PDF as sharp vector strokes.
* Fill PDF forms.
* Select text with copy, share, web search, translate, and dictionary actions.
* Share quotes as images, with the book's name and a choice of themes.
* Text Mode: read any PDF like an e-book, with reflow, fonts and themes.
* Dual page mode that pairs pages like an open book.
* Powerful search with live results and next/previous navigation inside the reader.
* Table of contents, user bookmarks, embedded links, and browser-like navigation history.
* Right-to-left reading, hide margins, auto-scroll, brightness control, zoom lock.
* Open password-protected and online PDFs, share and print.
* Incognito mode that saves nothing about what you read.
* Backup and restore your settings and reading progress.

<b>Permissions and privacy</b>

Your PDFs and reading data stay on your device. MJ PDF has no ads or tracking of any kind.

If the app crashes, you can choose to send a technical report to help diagnose it. The report contains a stack trace, app version, crash time, package name, Android version, phone model, logcat, and available memory. It does not include your PDF documents and is never sent without your approval.

The following permissions are required to provide specific features:

* Internet: open PDFs from links and send crash reports only if you approve.
* Storage / all-files access: discover, open, rename, delete, and manage PDFs across device and removable storage.
